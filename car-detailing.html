FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Rubbing & Polishing

Learn more about our rubbing and polishing services, how they restore your vehicle's paint, remove imperfections and bring back a deep, showroom-quality shine.

What is the difference between Normal Polishing and Sanding & Polishing?

−

Normal Polishing removes light swirl marks, oxidation and minor paint imperfections while restoring shine. Sanding & Polishing is a more advanced paint correction process that uses controlled wet sanding followed by multi-stage polishing to reduce deeper scratches, heavy oxidation and more noticeable paint defects.

Can polishing remove all scratches?

+

Polishing can significantly reduce or remove light scratches and swirl marks. However, deep scratches that have penetrated the paint or primer may require additional repair or repainting for complete restoration.

How long does the polishing effect last?

+

The results depend on driving conditions, washing methods and vehicle maintenance. Regular washing and proper paint protection help maintain the gloss and finish for a longer period.

Will polishing damage my vehicle's paint?

+

No. When performed by trained professionals using the correct compounds, polishing pads and techniques, the process safely restores your vehicle's finish while preserving the integrity of the paint.

Should I choose Normal Polishing or Sanding & Polishing?

+

If your vehicle has light swirl marks, dullness or minor imperfections, Normal Polishing is usually sufficient. For deeper scratches, oxidation and more severe paint defects, Sanding & Polishing delivers a higher level of paint correction and restoration.

Can I apply ceramic coating after polishing?

+

Yes. Polishing creates a clean, smooth paint surface, making it an excellent preparation step before applying ceramic coating or paint protection. This helps maximize the coating's bonding, durability and overall appearance.
