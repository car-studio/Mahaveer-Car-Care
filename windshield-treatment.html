FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Windshield Treatment

Find answers to the most common questions about our professional Windshield Treatment service, including glass protection, water repellency, durability and maintenance.

What is Windshield Treatment?

−

Windshield Treatment is a professional glass restoration and protection service that removes water spots, road grime and contaminants before applying a hydrophobic coating that improves visibility and repels water.

How long does the hydrophobic coating last?

+

The durability depends on driving conditions and maintenance. With proper care, the protective coating can continue providing excellent water repellency and glass protection for several months.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium Windshield Treatment?

+

Standard Windshield Treatment focuses on deep glass cleaning and water spot removal to restore visibility. Premium Windshield Treatment also includes an advanced hydrophobic coating for enhanced rain repellency, reduced glare and long-lasting protection.

Will this treatment improve driving in the rain?

+

Yes. The hydrophobic coating causes rainwater to bead and roll off the windshield more quickly, improving visibility and making driving safer during wet weather.

Can Windshield Treatment remove hard water stains?

+

Yes. Our professional treatment removes stubborn hard water stains, mineral deposits and road film, restoring the clarity and appearance of your windshield.

How should I maintain my treated windshield?

+

Use a quality glass cleaner, replace worn wiper blades when necessary and avoid abrasive cleaning products. Regular maintenance helps preserve the protective coating and maintain crystal-clear visibility.
