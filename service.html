EXTERIOR WASH PROCESS

Every Step Designed to Deliver a Spotless Finish.

Every exterior wash follows a carefully designed professional process that safely removes dirt, protects your paintwork and restores your vehicle's showroom-quality shine.

01
🧼

Pre-Wash Inspection

Every vehicle is inspected to identify heavy dirt, road contaminants, bug stains and sensitive paint areas before washing begins.

02
🫧

Premium Foam Application

A thick layer of premium pH-balanced foam loosens dirt, dust and road grime safely, preparing the surface for a scratch-free wash.

03
🧽

Scratch-Free Hand Wash

Soft microfiber wash mitts and the two-bucket method gently remove dirt while protecting your vehicle's paintwork.

04
🛞

Wheel & Tyre Cleaning

Dedicated brushes and premium cleaners remove brake dust, mud and road grime to restore a clean and refreshed appearance.

05
💨

Spot-Free Drying

High-quality microfiber drying towels and careful techniques eliminate water spots, leaving a streak-free finish.

06
✨

Final Quality Inspection

Every vehicle undergoes a final inspection to ensure exceptional cleanliness, enhanced gloss and a showroom-ready finish.
