FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Headlight Restoration

Learn more about our Headlight Restoration service, including the restoration process, durability, maintenance and how it improves both visibility and your vehicle's appearance.

Why do headlights become cloudy or yellow?

−

Headlights become cloudy due to prolonged exposure to UV rays, heat, road debris and environmental contaminants. Over time, the protective outer layer deteriorates, causing oxidation, yellowing and reduced clarity.

Can Headlight Restoration improve nighttime visibility?

+

Yes. Restoring the headlight lens allows more light to pass through, improving road visibility and making nighttime driving safer and more comfortable.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium Headlight Restoration?

+

Standard Headlight Restoration is ideal for removing light oxidation and restoring clarity. Premium Headlight Restoration includes deeper restoration, wet sanding when required, multi-stage polishing and enhanced UV protection for heavily oxidized headlights.

How long will the restoration last?

+

The durability depends on driving conditions, weather exposure and maintenance. Our UV protective coating helps extend the clarity of the headlights, and regular care can further prolong the results.

Is it better to restore headlights or replace them?

+

In many cases, restoration is a cost-effective solution for headlights affected by oxidation and surface damage. However, if the headlight housing or internal components are physically damaged, replacement may be the better option.

How often should headlights be restored?

+

There is no fixed schedule, as it depends on vehicle usage and environmental exposure. If your headlights become cloudy, yellow or noticeably dim, a professional restoration can help restore their performance and appearance.
