CERAMIC COATING PROCESS

Every Step Designed to Protect and Enhance Your Paint.

Our ceramic coating process follows a meticulous step-by-step application method to ensure maximum bonding, exceptional gloss and long-lasting protection for your vehicle's paintwork.

01
🔍

Vehicle Inspection

Our experts carefully inspect your vehicle's paintwork to identify swirl marks, scratches, oxidation and surface imperfections before the coating process begins.

02
🧼

Surface Preparation

The vehicle is thoroughly washed and decontaminated to remove dirt, tar, iron particles and other contaminants, creating the ideal surface for ceramic coating.

03
✨

Paint Correction

Minor swirl marks and surface imperfections are corrected through professional polishing to achieve a smooth, flawless finish before coating application.

04
🛡️

Ceramic Coating Application

Our premium ceramic coating is carefully applied panel by panel, creating a durable protective layer that bonds with your vehicle's paint for long-lasting performance.

05
💧

Curing & Bonding

The coating is allowed to cure properly, ensuring maximum bonding strength, superior hydrophobic performance and enhanced durability.

06
✅

Final Quality Inspection

Every coated surface is thoroughly inspected to ensure a flawless finish, deep gloss and complete protection before the vehicle is delivered.
