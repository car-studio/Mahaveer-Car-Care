MAHAVEER CAR CARE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Mahaveer Car Care respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using this website and our car care services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through this website, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our online booking facility or contact us through this website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your vehicle model or vehicle-related information
  • Your preferred service date
  • Your preferred service time
  • Additional notes or information you choose to provide

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, arrange a booking, or provide the requested service.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information provided through our website may be used for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry.
  • To arrange or manage a service booking.
  • To contact you regarding your requested service.
  • To understand your vehicle and service requirements.
  • To provide customer support and follow-up communication.
  • To improve our website and customer experience.

4. Booking Information and WhatsApp

When you submit the booking form, the information you provide may be formatted into a booking message and sent to Mahaveer Car Care through WhatsApp for the purpose of handling your service request.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through the website booking form.

5. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your information may be shared with service providers or communication platforms where reasonably necessary to respond to your request, process a booking, provide customer support, or operate the website.

For example, booking information may be transmitted through WhatsApp when you choose to submit a booking request using the website's booking facility. Such third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services and resources such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome, Google Maps, WhatsApp, hosting services, or other technical services required to operate the website.

These third parties may process certain technical information or information necessary to provide their respective services. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply.

7. Website Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your enquiry, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal data, including the ability to request information about the personal data being processed, request correction of inaccurate information, and request deletion where applicable.

You may contact Mahaveer Car Care if you have a question about personal information that you have submitted through this website.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general customers seeking automotive services. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of providing automotive services.

11. External Links

This website may contain links or connections to third-party websites and services. Mahaveer Car Care is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technology, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact Mahaveer Car Care using the contact information provided on our website.
