FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Interior Cleaning

Find answers to the most common questions about our interior cleaning services, including normal cleaning, steam cleaning and odor removal.

What is included in Interior Normal Cleaning?

−

Interior Normal Cleaning includes vacuuming seats and carpets, dashboard cleaning, door panel cleaning, center console cleaning, interior glass cleaning and overall dust removal to keep your cabin neat and fresh.

What is the difference between Normal Cleaning and Steam Cleaning?

+

Normal Cleaning removes everyday dust and dirt, while Steam Cleaning provides deep sanitization using high-temperature steam to eliminate stubborn stains, bacteria and germs without harsh chemicals.

Can steam cleaning damage my car's interior?

+

No. Our trained professionals use controlled steam equipment and safe techniques that effectively clean and sanitize your interior without damaging seats, dashboards or other surfaces.

How does the Odor Removal service work?

+

We use professional odor-neutralizing treatments that eliminate odors at their source instead of simply masking them, leaving your cabin fresh and pleasant.

How long does the interior cleaning service take?

+

Service time depends on your vehicle's condition and the package selected. Most interior cleaning services are completed within 1 to 3 hours.

How often should I get my car's interior professionally cleaned?

+

For the best appearance and hygiene, we recommend professional interior cleaning every 3 to 6 months, or more frequently if your vehicle is used daily or carries children or pets.
